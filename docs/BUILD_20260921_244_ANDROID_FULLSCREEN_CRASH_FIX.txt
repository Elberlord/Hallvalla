HallValla v244 — Android v139 fullscreen startup crash fix
Date: 2026-09-21

Root cause observed on physical HONOR / Android 15
- java.lang.NullPointerException while obtaining WindowInsetsController during MainActivity startup.
- The failing fullscreen request happened before the DecorView/insets controller was ready.

Fix
- No immersive-mode call before setContentView().
- Fullscreen configuration begins after content is mounted.
- Immersive-mode work is deferred through View.post().
- Window, DecorView and WindowInsetsController are null-checked.
- Safe reapplication on resume/focus.
- sensorLandscape retained: horizontal only, either landscape side.

Android
- applicationId: com.hallvalla.game
- versionCode: 139
- versionName: 1.0.139
- compileSdk/targetSdk: 35

Scope
- No gameplay changes.
- No Firebase Rules changes.
- No frontend layout changes.
- No asset changes.
- Private signing material must remain outside the repository.
